# Contributing to MagicBrain

Thank you for helping improve MagicBrain.

Before opening a pull request, read:

- `LICENSING.md`
- `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md`
- `CLA.md`
- `SECURITY.md`
- `PATENTS.md`
- `TRADEMARKS.md`

## Contribution Rules

- Keep public contributions limited to source code, public documentation,
  public examples, tests, and reproducible non-confidential evidence.
- Do not submit secrets, `.env` values, private prompts, private datasets,
  filing drafts, claim language, attorney work product, or customer data.
- Include a short rationale, changed files summary, and validation steps.
- Keep changes focused; avoid mixing unrelated refactors with feature work.
- Confirm that your contribution can be distributed under the dual
  `AGPL-3.0-only` + commercial model described in `CLA.md`.

## Patent-Sensitive Work

For genome-driven neurogenesis, digital twins, substrate metrics, adaptation
loops, or related commercial logic, discuss disclosure boundaries privately
before publishing implementation details.

Contact: `uwt@xteam.pro`
