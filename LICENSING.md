# MagicBrain Licensing

MagicBrain uses a dual-licensing model:

- **Open-source route:** GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, full text in
  [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **Commercial route:** proprietary commercial license, baseline terms in
  [COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

## Open-source route

Use MagicBrain under AGPL-3.0 if you are willing to comply with AGPL obligations,
including source availability requirements for network-accessible modified
versions where applicable.

## Commercial route

Use the commercial route if you need proprietary, SaaS, embedded, enterprise,
white-label, paid research, or production deployment rights without AGPL
obligations.

## Patent and trade-secret boundary

MagicBrain includes patent-sensitive research areas such as genome-driven neural
architecture generation, neurogenesis, digital twins, and substrate metrics.
Public releases should not include confidential filing drafts, private fitness
functions, exact production thresholds, private telemetry, or customer-specific
integrations.

For commercial licensing: `uwt@xteam.pro`.
