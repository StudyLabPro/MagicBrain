# MagicBrain Code of Conduct

MagicBrain is intended to be a serious, curious, and respectful research and
product community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections quickly.
- Critique ideas, code, data, and claims without attacking people.
- Keep security, legal, and patent-sensitive discussions private when needed.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private security reports, confidential patent material, secrets,
  private datasets, or personal data.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or research discussions.

Report concerns to `uwt@xteam.pro`.
